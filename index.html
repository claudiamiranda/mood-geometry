where this comes from

mood geometry began as an étude in cosmic things, a course on blender and spatial storytelling i taught at gray area in san francisco in 2026. the assignment was simple: take one modeled object and distort it three ways, each toward a different mood.

the exercise asks you to make a feeling visible before you can explain it, and to notice how little it takes for the same form to read as something else. this is that étude, made small enough to do alone, on a phone, in a few minutes.
